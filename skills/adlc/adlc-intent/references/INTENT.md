# Intent artifact

Adapt headings to an existing project convention; keep every section's meaning.

```markdown
---
adlc: intent
slug: <slug>
status: draft
---

# <Outcome-oriented title>

> Drafted by an agent from an interview with <human>; the human's answers are authoritative.

## Problem

The current situation and its pain, in the human's own words.

## Outcome

What is better when this is done. An outcome, not a solution.

## Who

The people and systems affected.

## Why now

The evidence, cost, risk, or opportunity that makes this worth doing now.

## Non-goals

What this work will not do, and why.

## Success signal

The observable signal that the outcome was reached, and how it will be read.

## Assumptions

- <assumption> — owner: <who confirms it> — decide by: <spec | plan>

## Open questions

- <question> — owner: <who answers it> — decide by: <spec | plan>

## Approvals
```
