# Agile Sprint Review

`agile-sprint-review` decides what product value was actually delivered.

- **Invocation:** model-invoked for sprint reviews, demonstrations, stakeholder acceptance, or outcome-based delivery review.
- **Output:** evidence-backed acceptance decisions and resulting backlog candidates.

The skill pins the intended outcome and exact implementation state, exercises working behavior through the most public seam available, and keeps reported results separate from checks performed during the review.

Each outcome becomes Accepted, Partially accepted, Not accepted, or Pending stakeholder decision. Passing tests never substitutes for stakeholder acceptance. New demand and rejected scope become separate backlog candidates rather than silently extending completed work.

This is product review. Engineering standards, maintainability, and specification compliance remain code-review concerns.
