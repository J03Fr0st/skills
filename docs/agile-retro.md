# Agile Retro

`agile-retro` converts delivery evidence into one measurable improvement experiment.

- **Invocation:** model-invoked after a cycle, incident, release, or meaningful milestone.
- **Output:** an evidence-based retrospective and one bounded experiment in the cycle record.

The skill reconstructs material scope changes, delays, interruptions, failures, recoveries, defects, review feedback, and prior experiments. It separates observable facts from effects and causal hypotheses before selecting a leverage point the team can influence.

Every experiment has an owner, review point, observable success signal, and stop condition. Changes to working agreements, skills, or agent instructions remain proposals until a human approves them; one anecdote does not become a universal rule.
